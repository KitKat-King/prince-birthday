# Perplexity Output

## Summary
- **Model**: Perplexity AI
- **Date**: 2026-11-14
- **Approach**: Pure, single-file Next.js App Router experience with Framer Motion and Tailwind CSS. Focuses on an ultra-minimalist, high-contrast monochrome aesthetic (pitch black, pure white, glassmorphism, radial glow).

## Features
1. **Screen-Takeover Entrance Gate**: Black modal gate with pulsing radial glow, "18 · 11 · 2010", blurred-to-sharp "PRINCE" title, and a shimmer button with sweep animation to open the celebration.
2. **Atmosphere**: Giant 85vh radial ambient glow with scroll-driven scale and opacity, plus 16 floating SVG lilies with random positions and swaying motion.
3. **Hero Section**: Scroll-driven scaling and fading hero with clamp typography, "Born on 18 November 2010", and an animated scroll indicator.
4. **"The Birthday File" (Age / Facts)**: 3D tilt cards for Name, Birthday, Color (Pitch Black), and Flower (Lily).
5. **Soundtrack of Prince (Music Player)**: Glassmorphic player with equalizer bars, spinning lily disc, progress bar with elapsed timer, play/pause controls, and selectable track buttons for *Bairan* and *Tere Aasa Pass Din Sara*.
6. **"A Letter For You"**: Message cards with border accents celebrating Prince's milestones.
7. **Finale**: Giant 80vw rotating background lily behind "STAY LEGENDARY" typography and anniversary badges.
